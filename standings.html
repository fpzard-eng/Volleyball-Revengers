🏆 Official National Tournament (NT) Format
Stage 1: Qualification Round
Top 36 Clubs compete in a Single-Elimination Tournament (Best of 3 sets: 25-25-15). 16 teams survive to advance.
Stage 2: Round of 16
16 teams enter a random draw pool for 8 knockout matches (Best of 3 sets: 25-25-15). 8 teams survive to reach the Finals.
Stage 3: Finals (Elite Eight)
Best of 5 sets (FIVB / NCAA style). Features Quarterfinals, Semifinals, 3rd Place Match (SF Losers), and National Championship Match.
