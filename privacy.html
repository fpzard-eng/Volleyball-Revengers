Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Mugen Revolution Studios ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you play Volleyball Revengers ("the Game") on Meta Quest and related platforms.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver a secure, performant multiplayer virtual reality experience, we process limited technical and account data:

  • Account Identifiers: Meta platform IDs, internal account tokens, and display names used to identify players and manage authentication.
  • Game & Gameplay Data: In-game statistics, matchmaking parameters, match histories, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Network & Performance Data: IP addresses, latency, and basic telemetry data to ensure server stability, synchronize multiplayer states, and troubleshoot connectivity issues.

2. How We Use Information

Collected data is strictly utilized to operate and enhance the core functionality of the Game, specifically for:

  • Authenticating player identity and saving progression state in cloud storage.
  • Managing low-latency peer-to-peer and server networking for real-time physics and multiplayer synchronization.
  • Providing mixed reality and virtual camera composition integration during gameplay capture.
  • Maintaining service integrity, enforcing fair play, and preventing exploit attempts.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with industry-standard third-party service providers to handle backend execution, networking, and content capture. These providers handle data in accordance with their respective privacy standards:

  • Microsoft PlayFab: Used for secure cloud save management, player profile storage, leaderboards, and title backend authentication.
  • Photon Fusion (Exit Games): Powers networking infrastructure, state synchronization, and low-latency multiplayer session management.
  • LIV Inc.: Utilized to support avatar integration, mixed-reality capture, and external virtual camera mechanics.

We do not sell, rent, or trade player data to third-party advertisers or commercial entities.

4. Data Retention & Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational precautions to protect player information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Gameplay data and account records are retained only for as long as necessary to maintain active service operations for Volleyball Revengers.
